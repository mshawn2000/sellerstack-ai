SmartScout alternatives

Short answer
There's no exact SmartScout clone at its price. Pick by job instead. For quick wholesale/OA product checks, SellerAmp SAS costs $19.95–$49.95/mo. For private-label product research, Jungle Scout starts at $49/mo ($29/mo yearly). For keyword-led research, Helium 10 Platinum is $129/mo ($99/mo yearly). If brand-level mapping is the job, SmartScout itself starts at $29/mo yearly, which may be cheaper than switching.
Last checked
2026-10-14 · By Shawn Mai, AI Seller Index

We have not tested these tools hands-on; this page is built from each vendor's own pricing and feature pages plus our tool listings. Links on this page are plain (non-affiliate) links. Always confirm prices on the vendor site.

What job did you use SmartScout for?

  • Brand/wholesale lead finding: SmartScout itself, or Seller 365
  • Product scanning / ROI checks: SellerAmp SAS
  • Private-label research: Jungle Scout or Helium 10
