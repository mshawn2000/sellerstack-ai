sellerboard alternatives

Short answer
sellerboard's own entry plan is already low ($19/mo monthly, $15/mo yearly, up to 3,000 orders), so most switches are about fit, not price. At very low volume, Shopkeeper Starter ($20/mo, up to 250 orders) and Sellerise Starter ($19.99/mo, up to 1,000 orders) price in the same range. If you also want keyword research and ads in one bill, Helium 10 Diamond includes profit and loss reporting, at $359/mo.

Stay on sellerboard if…

  • You sell mainly on Amazon and only need profit numbers.
  • You are under 3,000 orders/mo: Standard is $15/mo on yearly billing.
  • The basic UI doesn't bother you.

FAQ

Cheapest option at 1,000 orders/mo?

From the listed tiers: sellerboard Standard ($19/mo, $15/mo yearly, up to 3,000 orders), Sellerise Starter ($19.99/mo, up to 1,000 orders) and Shopkeeper Intermediate ($45/mo, up to 1,000 orders).

Is there a free sellerboard alternative?

None of the tools on this page lists a free plan for profit tracking; sellerboard, Sellerise and Shopkeeper offer free trials.
