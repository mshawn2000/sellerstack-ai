Helium 10 alternatives

Short answer
Helium 10 Platinum is $129/mo ($99/mo yearly), so the cheaper alternatives replace one job, not the whole suite. Product research: Jungle Scout Starter ($49/mo, $29 yearly). Keywords and listings: Datadive Starter ($39/mo, $32 yearly) or SellerSprite Basic ($39/mo). Brand/wholesale mapping: SmartScout Basic ($49/mo, $29 yearly). Profit only: sellerboard ($19/mo, $15 yearly).
Last checked
2026-10-14 · By Shawn Mai, AI Seller Index

We have not tested these tools hands-on; this page is built from each vendor's own pricing and feature pages plus our tool listings. Links on this page are plain (non-affiliate) links. Always confirm prices on the vendor site.

Helium 10 also has a limited Free plan, which may be enough if you only need occasional lookups.
