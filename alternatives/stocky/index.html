Stocky alternatives for Shopify

Short answer
Per Shopify's help center, Stocky stopped working on August 31, 2026 (APIs too), with read-only export access for at least 90 days. Basic purchase orders and transfers now live in the Shopify admin at no extra cost. If you relied on Stocky's forecasting, Prediko starts at $49/mo under $100K GMV with a 14-day trial; Inventory Planner is quote-only. Export your Stocky purchase-order history first — Shopify says it can't be imported, and suppliers can't be exported.

FAQ

Can I still get my Stocky data?

Shopify says you have read-only export access for at least 90 days after Aug 31, 2026. Don't uninstall Stocky first.

Do my old purchase orders move to Shopify?

No. Shopify says historical Stocky purchase orders can't be imported; export them for your records.
