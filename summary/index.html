Product Research

  • Helium 10: best for All-in-one Amazon suite (research, keywords, listings, PPC); for Sellers wanting one subscription for most tasks; limitation: Broad but deep tiers get pricey; learning curve; pricing: Platinum $129/mo ($99/mo billed yearly); Diamond/Enterprise higher.
  • SmartScout: best for Brand/seller-level market mapping and wholesale sourcing; for Wholesale and brand-analysis focused sellers; limitation: Less useful for private-label keyword work; pricing: $49/$119/$299 monthly; $29/$99/$239 per month billed yearly.
