Best AI Customer Service tools for sellers

It depends on size: Gorgias — E-com helpdesk with AI agent and Shopify integration. Alternatives: Tidio, Yuma AI, Zipchat, eDesk.

Gorgias

Best for: E-com helpdesk with AI agent and Shopify integration
Who: Shopify brands with real ticket volume
Limitations: Ticket-based pricing adds up

Tidio

Best for: Chat widget plus Lyro AI agent
Who: Small Shopify stores
Limitations: AI usage billed separately

Yuma AI

Best for: Autonomous AI support agent on top of helpdesks
Who: High-volume Shopify brands
Limitations: Needs an existing helpdesk

Zipchat

Best for: AI sales/support chat for Shopify
Who: Stores wanting pre-sale conversion chat
Limitations: Smaller vendor

eDesk

Best for: Marketplace-native helpdesk (Amazon, eBay, Shopify)
Who: Multi-marketplace sellers
Limitations: Less suited to pure DTC
