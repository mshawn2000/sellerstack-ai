Best AI Product Research tools for sellers

It depends on size: Jungle Scout — Amazon market intelligence and product validation. Alternatives: Helium 10, SmartScout, Viral Launch, SellerSprite, Keepa, SellerApp.

Jungle Scout

Best for: Amazon market intelligence and product validation
Who: Sellers $0–$1M+ and brands needing 1P/3P data
Limitations: Estimates are modeled, not actual sales

Helium 10

Best for: All-in-one Amazon suite (research, keywords, listings, PPC)
Who: Sellers wanting one subscription for most tasks
Limitations: Broad but deep tiers get pricey; learning curve

SmartScout

Best for: Brand/seller-level market mapping and wholesale sourcing
Who: Wholesale and brand-analysis focused sellers
Limitations: Less useful for private-label keyword work

Viral Launch

Best for: Product discovery plus keyword research
Who: Private-label beginners
Limitations: Overlaps heavily with Jungle Scout/Helium 10

SellerSprite

Best for: Low-cost keyword and product research across marketplaces
Who: Budget-conscious and non-US marketplace sellers
Limitations: UI/translation rough in places

Keepa

Best for: Price/BSR history tracking
Who: Arbitrage, wholesale and every FBA seller checking history
Limitations: Not AI-driven; data-heavy charts

SellerApp

Best for: Research plus PPC automation in one
Who: Small brands wanting combined research + ads
Limitations: Jack of all trades
