AI PPC & Ads tools

Perpetua

Best for: Goal-based Amazon/Walmart ad automation
Who: Brands spending meaningful monthly ad budgets
Limitations: Less granular manual control

Teikametrics

Best for: AI bid optimization tied to inventory/profit
Who: Growing Amazon & Walmart brands
Limitations: Pricing scales with ad spend

Ad Badger

Best for: Rules-based Amazon PPC bid automation
Who: Small sellers who want hands-on control
Limitations: Older UI; less AI than rivals

Quartile

Best for: Managed + AI ad optimization across retail channels
Who: Larger brands/agencies
Limitations: Enterprise-oriented

Pacvue

Best for: Enterprise retail media management
Who: Big brands and agencies
Limitations: Overkill and costly for small sellers

Scale Insights

Best for: Template-based Amazon PPC automation
Who: Sellers comfortable with rule logic
Limitations: Setup takes effort
