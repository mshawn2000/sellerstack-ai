Best tools for Amazon sellers on Black Friday & Cyber Monday

Short answer
Big sales days can look great in Seller Central and still lose money once deal costs, coupons, ad spend and refunds come in. For most Amazon sellers the one tool to have running before Black Friday is a profit tracker: sellerboard ($19/mo, or $15/mo billed yearly) shows real profit per day and per product after Amazon fees, PPC and your item cost. If you resell items with many sellers on the listing, a repricer like BQool (from $25/mo) helps you keep the Buy Box when prices move fast. RestockPro (from $49/mo) helps you plan what to send to FBA so you do not run out in December, and FeedbackFive (from $34/mo) sends review requests for all the extra orders. Shopify stores can use Profit Calc (from $39/mo) to see profit after ad spend. Prices were read from each vendor's site between Oct 14 and Nov 26, 2026.

FAQ

Why can Black Friday sales lose money on Amazon?

Deal fees, coupon costs, lower prices, higher ad spend and more returns all cut into profit. Seller Central shows sales, but not your item cost, so you only see the real result in a profit tracker.

When should I set up these tools?

Before the sale if you can, so your item costs are entered and the numbers are ready. Some tools need a day or more to load your Amazon data after you connect.

Do I need a repricer for Black Friday?

Only if you share listings with other sellers (resale, wholesale, arbitrage). Private label brands that own their listing usually do not need one.

Is it OK to ask buyers for reviews after holiday sales?

Yes, if you use Amazon's own Request a Review option or tools built on it. Do not offer anything in return for a review.

Which tool should a small seller pick first?

A profit tracker. Knowing which products made money on Black Friday tells you what to restock, what to advertise and what to drop.
