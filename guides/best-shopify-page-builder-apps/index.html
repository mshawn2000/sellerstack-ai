Best Shopify page builder apps

Short answer
A page builder lets you design landing pages, product pages and theme sections without editing theme code. PageFly is the cheapest paid start: Free covers 1 published slot (one page or one section) with 10 AI credits a month, and Builder is $24/mo for 5 published pages or sections ($5 per extra 5 slots) plus dashboard analytics. Accelerate is $99/mo or $990/yr for unlimited pages, A/B tests, heatmaps and AI Page Checkup; Enterprise is $299/mo. GemPages is built around sales funnels. Free publishes 1 page and includes the funnel and post-purchase upsell tools; Build is $29/mo for unlimited published pages and 20 theme sections; Optimize is $59/mo for 100 theme sections, scheduled publishing, a headless page option and unlimited post-purchase upsell volume; Enterprise is $199/mo. Shogun's Draft Mode is free but cannot publish live pages. Build is $39/mo or $372/yr for 25 published pages; Grow is $199/mo or $1,908/yr for unlimited pages, scheduling and global sections; Advanced is $499/mo or $4,788/yr and adds content syncing between stores. Paid Shogun plans list a 10-day free trial. A/B testing is a separate Shogun app, not part of these plans. Prices were read from each app's Shopify App Store page.

Quick picks

  • Free plan that can publish one page: PageFly Free, 1 published slot (page or section) and 10 AI credits/mo. GemPages Free also publishes 1 page.
  • Cheapest paid start: PageFly Builder, $24/mo for 5 published pages or sections.
  • Unlimited published pages on a budget: GemPages Build, $29/mo.
  • Visual builder, draft first: Shogun Draft Mode is free (no publishing); Build is $39/mo for 25 live pages.
  • Most reviews among these three: PageFly, 4.9 from about 5,922 reviews.

FAQ

Is there a free Shopify page builder?

Yes. PageFly Free lets you publish 1 page or section. GemPages Free lets you publish 1 page. Shogun Draft Mode is free but you cannot publish live pages until you pay.

Which is cheapest if I need more than one live page?

PageFly Builder at $24/mo (5 slots). If you need unlimited live pages, GemPages Build at $29/mo is cheaper than Shogun Grow at $199/mo.

Do these replace my Shopify theme?

No. They sit on top of your theme so you can build landing pages, product pages and sections. Your theme still runs the rest of the store.

Does Shogun include A/B testing?

No. The Shopify listing says A/B testing is in a separate Shogun app. PageFly includes A/B tests on Accelerate ($99/mo). GemPages includes offer A/B testing in its sales funnel tools.
