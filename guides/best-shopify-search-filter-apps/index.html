Best Shopify search & filter apps

Short answer
Shopify's own Search & Discovery app is free, and it is enough for many small stores. If shoppers miss products because of typos or weak filters, a search app can help. Searchanise is the cheapest paid start we checked: $19/mo for up to 1,500 products, or free if you have fewer than 25 products (or a dev store). Fast Simon also has a free plan, but it only covers 100 sessions a month, so most live stores will need Starter at $39.99/mo ($399.96/yr). Boost AI Search & Filter starts lower, from $29/mo, but its price is based on monthly sales and can rise to $159/mo even under $50K GMV; it has a 21-day free trial and no free plan on the App Store page we checked. Prices were read from each app's Shopify App Store page.

Quick picks

  • Cheapest paid plan: Searchanise Basic at $19/mo or $192/yr, for up to 1,500 products, with a 14-day free trial.
  • Free plan for a tiny catalog: Searchanise Free, for dev stores and stores with fewer than 25 products.
  • Free plan to try AI instant search: Fast Simon Free: 100 monthly sessions, then Starter at $39.99/mo for 2,000 sessions.
  • Price starts at $29/mo, scales with sales: Boost Launch, from $29/mo up to $159/mo under $50K monthly GMV, with a 21-day free trial.
  • Built-in and free: Shopify's own Search & Discovery app, which is free. Start there if the built-in search already works for you.

FAQ

Is there a free Shopify search app?

Yes. Shopify's own Search & Discovery app is free. Searchanise is free for dev stores and stores with fewer than 25 products. Fast Simon has a free plan, but only for 100 sessions a month.

What is the cheapest paid Shopify search app?

Of the prices we checked, Searchanise Basic is the lowest at $19/mo ($192/yr) for up to 1,500 products. Boost starts at $29/mo, and Fast Simon Starter is $39.99/mo.

Does Boost stay at $29 a month?

Not necessarily. Boost's price is based on your monthly online GMV. Launch is from $29/mo up to $159/mo for stores under $50K GMV, and higher tiers start at $299/mo and $699/mo.

When do I need a search app?

If shoppers search and get poor results, or cannot filter a large catalog, a search app can help. If your catalog is small and Shopify's built-in search works, you may not need one yet.
