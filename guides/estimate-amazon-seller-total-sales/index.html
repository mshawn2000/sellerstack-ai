How to estimate an Amazon seller's total sales

Short answer
No tool sees a seller's real sales; every number is an estimate. The free method: open the seller's storefront, estimate each top ASIN's monthly units with a product sales estimator, and add them up. For a seller-level view, SmartScout lists Seller Research and Seller History in its plans ($49 / $119 / $299 a month; $29–$239 yearly) — which tier includes seller-level data we did not confirm. Jungle Scout's brand and competitor views are in Cobalt (custom pricing) or Brand Owner.

The manual method

  1. Open the seller's storefront and list their ASINs.
  2. Estimate monthly units for the top ASINs with any product sales estimator.
  3. Multiply by price and sum. Shared listings: the seller only gets part of the sales when others win the Buy Box.
  4. Treat the total as a range, not a number.

FAQ

How accurate are seller sales estimates?

Not very at the seller level: errors on each ASIN add up, and estimators can't see Buy Box sharing, off-Amazon sales or returns. Use them to compare sellers, not to value a business.

Is there a free seller-sales estimator?

We found none we could verify. The storefront + product-estimate method is free if you already have a product estimator.
