Cheapest Amazon profit tracker

Short answer
At up to 3,000 orders a month, sellerboard Standard is the lowest price we verified: $19/mo, or $15/mo ($179/yr) billed yearly, with a 1-month free trial. Under 250 orders, Shopkeeper Starter ($20/mo) is similar; under 1,000 orders, Sellerise Starter is $19.99/mo. Helium 10's full profit and loss reporting is on Diamond ($359/mo), so it is not the cheap route.
