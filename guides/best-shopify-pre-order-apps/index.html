Best Shopify pre-order apps

Short answer
A pre-order app swaps the Sold out or Add to cart button for a Pre-order button, shows when the item ships, and can take a deposit now and the rest later. Timesact has the most generous free plan: 20 pre-orders a month on unlimited products, with unlimited emails and Klaviyo, but it shows Timesact branding. CORE Flex starts at $5/mo for 10 extra orders, then $0.20 per order above the limit, and adds split payments and deposits; PRO Flex starts at $7/mo and adds Markets & Locations and charging when you fulfill. If you sell a lot of pre-orders, CORE Fixed gives unlimited orders from $23/mo on Basic Shopify ($204/yr), with the price rising on bigger Shopify plans. PreOrder Globo is the simplest flat price: Free covers your first 20 presale orders and first 100 restock emails in total, Lite is $9.90/mo ($99/yr) for 100 presales and 1,000 restock emails a month with partial payments, and PRO is $19.90/mo ($199/yr) for unlimited presales and 5,000 restock emails. PreProduct is built for big launches: Starter is free to install but takes 5% of paid pre-order revenue; Scale is $99.99/mo with 0% commission on your first $5k of monthly pre-order revenue (0.5% after) plus API and Shopify Flow access; Scale Plus is $259.99/mo with 0% commission. Prices were read from each app's Shopify App Store page.

Quick picks

  • Free plan for a few pre-orders a month: Timesact Free, 20 orders a month on unlimited products (with Timesact branding).
  • Cheapest unlimited pre-orders: PreOrder Globo PRO, $19.90/mo or $199/yr.
  • Pay only when you sell: PreProduct Starter, free to install plus 5% of paid pre-order revenue.
  • Deposits and split payments: All three. Timesact needs CORE Flex ($5/mo) or higher; Globo needs Lite ($9.90/mo); PreProduct includes deposits on Starter.
  • Big launch with API and Shopify Flow: PreProduct Scale, $99.99/mo.

FAQ

Can Shopify do pre-orders without an app?

Shopify's Continue selling when out of stock setting lets people buy at zero stock, but it keeps the normal Add to cart button and does not tell buyers when the item ships. A pre-order app adds the Pre-order button, ship-date message and payment options like deposits.

Which pre-order app is free?

Timesact Free gives 20 orders a month. PreOrder Globo Free covers only your first 20 presale orders in total. PreProduct Starter is free to install but takes 5% of paid pre-order revenue.

When does PreProduct's 5% fee cost more than a paid plan?

5% of $2,000 a month in pre-order sales is $100, about the price of PreProduct Scale ($99.99/mo). Above that, Scale is cheaper, and it is 0% commission up to $5k a month.

Do these apps also send back-in-stock alerts?

Timesact and PreOrder Globo both include back-in-stock alerts. PreProduct focuses on pre-orders and email campaigns for them.
