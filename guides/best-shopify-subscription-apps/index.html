Best Shopify subscription apps

Short answer
A subscription app lets customers subscribe and save, so they reorder on a schedule without coming back to your store. Seal Subscriptions is free up to 50 subscriptions, then $5.95/mo up to 100, $9.95/mo up to 250 and $24.95/mo up to 500, with a 0% transaction fee. Appstle Subscriptions is priced by subscription revenue instead: free up to $500 a month, $10/mo up to $5,000 and $30/mo up to $15,000, also with a 0% transaction fee, and adds build-a-box and bundles. Loop Subscriptions is built for brands with many subscribers: free up to 50 active subscriptions, then $99/mo plus 1.0% per transaction or $399/mo plus 0.75%. Prices were read from each app's Shopify App Store page.

Quick picks

  • Cheapest way to start: Seal Subscriptions, free up to 50 subscriptions and $5.95/mo up to 100.
  • Few subscribers but high order value: Appstle Subscriptions, priced by revenue, free up to $500/mo.
  • Build-a-box and bundles: Appstle Subscriptions Business plan, $30/mo.
  • Large subscriber base that needs retention tools: Loop Subscriptions, from $99/mo plus 1.0% per transaction.

FAQ

Which Shopify subscription app is free?

Seal Subscriptions and Loop Subscriptions are free up to 50 subscriptions. Appstle Subscriptions is free up to $500 a month in subscription revenue.

Do subscription apps take a cut of sales?

Seal and Appstle list a 0% transaction fee. Loop's paid plans add 1.0% (Starter) or 0.75% (Pro) per transaction.

Should I price by subscriptions or by revenue?

If you have many low-priced subscriptions, revenue-based pricing like Appstle can be cheaper. If you have few high-priced ones, a count-based plan like Seal can be cheaper.
