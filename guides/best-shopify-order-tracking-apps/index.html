Best Shopify order tracking apps

Short answer
An order tracking app gives your customers a branded page where they can see where their package is, and sends shipping updates for you. That cuts the 'where is my order' emails that fill up your inbox. All three apps below have a free plan and a 7-day free trial on paid plans. Track123 is the cheapest paid start: free for 50 orders a month, then Growth at $9/mo ($84/yr) for 300 orders. AfterShip is free for 50 shipments a month, then Essentials at $11/mo ($108/yr) for 100 shipments with email and SMS updates, and Premium at $70/mo puts the tracking page on your own domain. Parcel Panel (now called CWILL Order Tracking) is free for 20 orders a month, then Essential at $11/mo ($108/yr) for 200 orders, and adds shipping protection and discount upsells on the tracking page. Prices were read from each app's Shopify App Store page.

Quick picks

  • Cheapest paid plan: Track123 Growth, $9/mo ($84/yr) for 300 orders.
  • Best-known brand, email and SMS updates: AfterShip Essentials, $11/mo for 100 shipments.
  • Most orders for $11/mo: Parcel Panel Essential, 200 orders/month plus upsells and shipping protection.
  • Dropshipping store: Track123 or Parcel Panel, both have a dropshipping mode on the free plan.
  • Tracking page on your own domain: AfterShip Premium, $70/mo for 500 shipments.

FAQ

Is there a free Shopify order tracking app?

Yes. Track123 and AfterShip are free for 50 orders or shipments a month, and Parcel Panel is free for 20 orders a month.

What happens if I go over my plan's order limit?

Track123 and Parcel Panel charge $0.05 per extra order. AfterShip charges $0.08 per extra shipment on Essentials and $0.12 on Premium.

Do tracking apps really cut support emails?

That is the main reason stores use them: customers can check status on a tracking page and get updates by email, instead of writing to you.

Can I hide the app's branding?

On Track123 that needs Advanced ($49/mo). On Parcel Panel it needs Professional ($59/mo).
