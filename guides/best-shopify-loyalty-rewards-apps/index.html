Best Shopify loyalty and rewards apps

Short answer
A loyalty app gives customers points for buying, signing up and referring friends, and lets them trade points for discounts. The goal is more repeat orders from people who already bought once. All three apps below have a free plan. Smile.io is free for up to 200 orders a month, then Essential at $15/mo for 500 orders, Standard at $79/mo for 1,000 orders, and $199/mo for 2,500 orders with VIP tiers and points expiry. BON Loyalty is the cheapest path to VIP tiers: free for 150 orders a month, Starter $15/mo (300 orders), Basic $29/mo (500 orders), and Growth $129/mo ($1,236/yr) with unlimited orders and VIP tiers. LoyaltyLion has the biggest free plan at 400 orders a month, then jumps to Classic at $199/mo with VIP tiers and loyalty revenue reports. Prices were read from each app's Shopify App Store page.

Quick picks

  • Biggest free plan: LoyaltyLion Free, up to 400 orders a month.
  • Cheapest paid plan: Smile.io Essential or BON Loyalty Starter, both $15/mo.
  • Cheapest VIP tiers: BON Loyalty Growth, $129/mo with unlimited orders.
  • Best-known and easiest setup: Smile.io, rated 4.9 from 4,660 reviews on the Shopify App Store.
  • Hands-on setup and deep integrations: LoyaltyLion Classic, $199/mo.

FAQ

Is there a free Shopify loyalty app?

Yes. LoyaltyLion is free up to 400 orders a month, Smile.io up to 200, and BON Loyalty up to 150.

Which loyalty app has VIP tiers for the least money?

BON Loyalty Growth at $129/mo. On Smile.io VIP tiers are on the $199/mo plan, and on LoyaltyLion they start with Classic at $199/mo.

Do these work in a physical store too?

Yes, all three list Shopify POS support. On BON Loyalty POS needs the Basic plan ($29/mo) or higher.

Do they work with Klaviyo?

Yes. Smile.io includes 1 integration such as Klaviyo from Essential, BON Loyalty from Starter, and LoyaltyLion Classic includes unlimited integrations.
