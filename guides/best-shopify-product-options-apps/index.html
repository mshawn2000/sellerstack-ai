Best Shopify product options apps

Short answer
Shopify caps each product at 2,048 variants, and its built-in options can't take a text box, a file upload or an extra charge for gift wrap. A product options app adds those fields to the product page and passes the choices into the order. Globo Product Options, Variant and Easify Custom Product Options both have real free plans with unlimited option sets, products and orders, 15 option types and no watermark. To charge extra for an option you need a paid plan: Globo Premium is $9.9/mo (right now a BFCM offer of $4.95/mo for the first 3 months), and Easify's first paid plan is $9.99/mo ($95.90/yr). For a live preview of engraving or artwork on the product photo, Globo Advanced is $19.9/mo (BFCM offer $9.95/mo for 3 months) and Easify's $19.99/mo plan has it too. SC Product Options by Shop Circle has been around since 2012 but has no free plan for live stores: Basic is $14.99/mo ($149.99/yr) for unlimited options and file uploads, and you need the $39.99/mo plan ($399.99/yr) for priced add-ons, conditional logic and swatches. Prices were read from each app's Shopify App Store page.

Quick picks

  • Free product options with no watermark: Globo Free or Easify Free. Both give unlimited option sets, products and orders.
  • Cheapest way to charge for add-ons: Globo Premium, $9.9/mo ($4.95/mo for the first 3 months during the BFCM offer).
  • Live preview for engraving or custom prints: Globo Advanced ($19.9/mo) or Easify's $19.99/mo plan.
  • Shopify POS orders with options: Globo Advanced, which includes POS support.
  • Long track record: SC Product Options, launched in 2012, from $14.99/mo.

FAQ

Why not just use Shopify variants?

Shopify limits each product to 2,048 variants, and variants can't collect a customer's text or uploaded file. An options app adds those fields without creating extra variants.

Which product options app is free?

Globo Product Options and Easify both have free plans with unlimited option sets, products and orders. SC Product Options is only free on development stores.

Can I charge extra for an option like gift wrap?

Yes, but not on the free plans. Globo needs Premium ($9.9/mo), Easify needs its $9.99/mo plan, and SC Product Options needs its $39.99/mo plan.

Do the selected options show on the order?

Yes. Globo and Easify both list options in orders, packing slips and emails, even on their free plans.
