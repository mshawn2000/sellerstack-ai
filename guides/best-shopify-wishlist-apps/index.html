Best Shopify wishlist apps

Short answer
A wishlist lets shoppers save products they like and come back later. The real value is what happens next: a good wishlist app can email shoppers when a saved item drops in price or comes back in stock, and send that list to your email tool. The three apps below price in very different ways. Growave bundles wishlist with loyalty points and product reviews, and its Free plan covers 200 orders a month; Entry is $15/mo for 500 orders. Swish (formerly Wishlist King) charges by website sessions: Starter is $19/mo with 10,000 sessions included, then $0.001 per extra session, and Growth is $79/mo for 100,000 sessions. Swym Wishlist Plus charges by wishlist actions: Starter is $29.99/mo for 3,000 actions, Pro is $59.99/mo for 10,000 actions and adds Klaviyo, Attentive and Omnisend, and Premium is $99.99/mo. Swym gives about 17% off when you pay yearly. Swym and Swish have 30-day free trials; Growave has 14 days. Prices were read from each app's Shopify App Store page.

Quick picks

  • Free wishlist: Growave Free, which also includes loyalty points and product reviews (200 orders/month, Growave branding).
  • Cheapest wishlist-only app: Swish Starter at $19/mo with 10,000 website sessions included.
  • Price-drop and back-in-stock alerts: Swym Wishlist Plus Starter ($29.99/mo) lists both.
  • Klaviyo wishlist flows: Swym Wishlist Plus Pro ($59.99/mo) or Growave Entry ($15/mo, one integration such as Klaviyo).
  • Custom or complex theme: Swish, which includes free setup and onboarding on every plan.

FAQ

Is there a free Shopify wishlist app?

Yes. Growave has a free plan with a wishlist, loyalty points and product reviews for up to 200 orders a month. Swish and Swym Wishlist Plus have free trials but no free plan.

How do Shopify wishlist apps charge?

It differs. Growave charges by orders, Swish by website sessions, and Swym Wishlist Plus by wishlist actions per month. Check which number grows fastest for your store.

Can a wishlist app send price-drop emails?

Swym Wishlist Plus lists price-drop and stock alerts on its Starter plan, and Swish lists stock and price alerts.

Do I need a separate back-in-stock app?

Maybe not. Swym and Swish both list stock alerts for saved items. For alerts on any sold-out product, see our back in stock apps guide.
