Best online arbitrage tools for Amazon

Short answer
Online arbitrage means buying from retail sites and reselling on Amazon, so you need fast answers on fees, profit, sales rank and whether you are allowed to sell the item. RevSeller is the cheapest we list at $99.99 a year ($8.33/mo). SellerAmp SAS starts at $19.95/mo and is a popular all-round sourcing calculator. Seller Assistant App (from $29.99/mo, $24.99 billed yearly) adds restriction checks and price-list scanning. BuyBotPro (from £11.95/mo plus VAT) automates the buy or skip decision. Keepa (from €29/mo) shows price and rank history so you don't buy a spike. Once you are selling, sellerboard (from $15/mo yearly) tracks your real profit.

Quick picks

  • Cheapest: RevSeller, $99.99/yr ($8.33/mo) or $19.99/mo, 30-day free trial.
  • Best all-round calculator: SellerAmp SAS, $19.95, $29.95 or $49.95 per month.
  • Restriction checks and price lists: Seller Assistant App, Pro $29.99/mo ($24.99/mo yearly).

  • Price and rank history: Keepa, from €29/mo, free tier available.
  • Bulk supplier files: ScanUnlimited, limited free plan; Unlimited $70/mo.
  • Track real profit: sellerboard, from $15/mo yearly ($19 monthly).

FAQ

What is the cheapest online arbitrage tool?

Of the tools we list, RevSeller is lowest at $99.99 a year, about $8.33 a month, with a 30-day free trial.

Do I need Keepa for online arbitrage?

Most sellers use it. Keepa shows price and sales rank history, which helps you avoid buying a product right after a short price spike.

SellerAmp SAS or Seller Assistant App?

Both check profit, ROI and restrictions. SellerAmp SAS starts lower at $19.95/mo. Seller Assistant App starts at $29.99/mo and adds price-list scanning and team seats.
