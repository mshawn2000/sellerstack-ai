Best Shopify email and SMS marketing apps

Short answer
Email and text messages are how most Shopify stores win back carts and bring buyers back. The big apps all do that, but they price it differently. Klaviyo has a free plan for up to 250 profiles and 500 emails a month, then the price grows with your list. Omnisend also has a free plan, and its Standard plan was shown at $11.20/mo with a 30% intro discount; texts start at $0.007 each. Privy starts at $30/mo with 250 SMS credits and is strong on pop-ups that grow your list. Postscript is text-only: its Starter plan is $0/mo with a $49 monthly minimum spend and $0.009 per SMS, and Growth is $100/mo. Prices here were checked on each tool's own pricing page today.

Quick picks

  • Free to start, grows with you: Klaviyo, free up to 250 profiles and 500 emails/month.
  • Email plus SMS on a budget: Omnisend, free plan; Standard shown at $11.20/mo with a 30% intro discount.
  • Pop-ups to grow your list: Privy, from $30/mo with 250 SMS credits; 15-day free trial.
  • Text messages only: Postscript, Starter $0/mo with a $49 monthly minimum spend.

FAQ

Which Shopify email app is free?

Klaviyo and Omnisend both have free plans. Klaviyo's free plan covers up to 250 profiles and 500 email sends a month.

Do I need a separate SMS app?

Not always. Klaviyo, Omnisend and Privy all do email and SMS. Postscript is SMS only, for stores that want a text-first tool.

Why does my price go up?

Most of these apps charge by how many contacts you have, and SMS is paid per message on top.
