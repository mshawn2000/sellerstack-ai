Does Helium 10 charge 2% of your ad spend?

Short answer
Yes, on Diamond. Helium 10's help center says Diamond plans pay a 2% management fee on the total combined ad spend of every advertising profile set to Managed — not only the campaigns you run through Helium 10 Ads — with no minimum, regardless of feature use, and non-refundable. To avoid it, set profiles to Analytics-Only or Disabled before the spend happens. Diamond itself is $359/mo ($279/mo yearly); Platinum ($129/mo) does not include Helium 10 Ads.
Last checked

What the fee applies to

  • A "profile" is one ad account + one marketplace (account A in the US and in Mexico = two profiles).
  • Each profile can be Managed, Analytics-Only or Disabled. Only Managed profiles are charged.
  • The 2% is on all ad spend in Managed profiles, even campaigns you never touched in Helium 10.
  • Enterprise and other custom plans may be priced differently.

FAQ

Does Platinum pay the 2%?

No. Helium 10 says Platinum and lower legacy plans do not include Helium 10 Ads management, so there is no Ads fee.

Can I use Helium 10 Ads analytics without the fee?

Yes: Helium 10 says profiles set to Analytics-Only or Disabled are not charged. Switch them before ad spend occurs; the fee is non-refundable.

Is the fee only on campaigns I create in Helium 10?

No. Helium 10's help center says it applies to all ad spend within Managed profiles.

Is there a minimum?

Helium 10 says there is no minimum spend requirement or threshold.
