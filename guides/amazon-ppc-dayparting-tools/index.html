Best Amazon PPC dayparting tools

Short answer
Dayparting means your Amazon ads run only in the hours that sell, and pause or bid lower in the hours that just burn money. Amazon does not do this well on its own, so sellers use a tool. Off Hours is built for it: hour-by-hour schedules plus budget and bid rules, checked every 15 minutes, for a flat $149/mo per ad account with a 14-day free trial and no card. Scale Insights (from $78/mo) has a dayparting rule inside its wider PPC automation. AdLabs has a free plan, but dayparting is on its Pro plan at 2% of ad spend plus $120/mo. atom11 (from $499/mo) and Adbrew (from $799/mo) adjust bids by hour as part of full PPC suites for bigger budgets. After you cut dead hours, check that profit actually went up; sellerboard (from $15/mo yearly) shows profit per product after ads and fees.

Quick picks

  • Flat price, dayparting first: Off Hours, $149/mo per ad account, 15% off yearly, 14-day free trial.
  • Cheapest PPC suite with a dayparting rule: Scale Insights, from $78/mo (5 automated ASINs).
  • Free plan to start, dayparting on Pro: AdLabs, Pro is 2% of ad spend + $120/mo, 30-day free trial.
  • Hourly bids for $50K/mo budgets: atom11, Beginner $499/mo for ad spend up to $50K/mo.
  • Full agency-grade suite: Adbrew, Standard $799/mo or a % of ad spend, whichever is greater.
  • Check profit after changes: sellerboard, from $15/mo yearly ($19 monthly).

FAQ

What is dayparting on Amazon?

It means changing your ads by the hour: pausing campaigns or lowering bids in hours that rarely convert, and raising them in hours that do.

Does Amazon have dayparting built in?

Amazon Ads lets you schedule some campaigns, but sellers who want hour-by-hour rules across many campaigns usually use a tool like Off Hours, Scale Insights or atom11.

Is a % of ad spend or a flat fee better?

A flat fee, like Off Hours at $149/mo, stays the same as your spend grows. Percent-of-spend tools, like AdLabs Pro at 2% + $120/mo, cost more as you scale but include more features.
