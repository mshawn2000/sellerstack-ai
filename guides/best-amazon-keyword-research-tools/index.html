Best Amazon keyword research tools

Short answer
Good keywords decide whether shoppers find your listing and whether your PPC money is wasted. The cheapest option we list is Keyword Tool Dominator, which has a free tier and one-time payments from $9 for 30 days. MerchantWords is keyword data only, at $35/mo for one country. SellerSprite (from $39/mo) and Datadive (from $39/mo monthly) go deeper into competitor keywords and listing builds. AMZScout bundles keywords with product research for $59.99 a month or $399.99 a year. SmartScout ($29/mo billed yearly, and our code AISELLERINDEX takes 25% off your first 3 months) adds brand and traffic-graph data. Helium 10 Platinum ($99/mo yearly) is the big all-in-one suite.

Quick picks

  • Cheapest: Keyword Tool Dominator, free tier, then one-time $9 for 30 days or $39 for a year.
  • Keyword data only: MerchantWords, $35/mo for one country, $79/mo for all 11 marketplaces.
  • Competitor keyword digging: SellerSprite, from $39/mo.
  • Listing keyword builds: Datadive, from $39/mo (Starter, monthly).
  • Keywords plus product research: AMZScout AI Bundle, $59.99/mo or $399.99/yr.
  • Brand and traffic data: SmartScout, from $29/mo yearly; code AISELLERINDEX for 25% off 3 months.
  • All-in-one suite: Helium 10 Platinum, $129/mo or $99/mo billed yearly.

FAQ

What is the cheapest Amazon keyword tool?

Of the tools we list, Keyword Tool Dominator is cheapest: it has a free tier and paid access starts at a one-time $9 for 30 days.

Is MerchantWords still worth it?

If you only need keyword search data, yes. It costs $35/mo for one country and pulls its data from the Amazon search bar. It does not do product research or profit tracking.

Do I need Helium 10 for keywords?

No. Helium 10 is strong but costs $99/mo or more. Cheaper tools like SellerSprite, Datadive or MerchantWords cover keyword research for less.
