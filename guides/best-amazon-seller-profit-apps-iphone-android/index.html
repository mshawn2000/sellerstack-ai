Best Amazon seller apps to track profit on your phone

Short answer
Amazon's own Amazon Seller app shows sales and orders, but not your true profit after product cost (COGS), PPC, refunds and every fee. For that you need a profit tool that also has a phone app. All four below have free iPhone and Android apps; you pay for the account behind them. QMT is the cheapest: free up to 1,000 orders a month with unlimited stores, and Pro is $200 a year with no order limit. Sellerview Basic is $15/mo billed yearly (up to 3,000 orders, 60 days of history); Complete is $79/mo yearly or $99/mo monthly with unlimited orders and an AI Copilot you can ask about your numbers. sellerboard starts at $19/mo or $15/mo billed yearly with a 2-month free trial and no card; its app shows net profit, PPC, stock and refunds, and is rated 4.7 on the App Store (400 ratings). tool4seller Startup is $199 a year (1,000 orders/mo) and adds PPC automation, hijacking alerts and review emails. Prices were read from each vendor's own site on Nov 21 to 22, 2026.

Quick picks

  • Free: QMT: free up to 1,000 orders/mo, unlimited stores. Pro is $200/yr.
  • Best all-round profit app: sellerboard, from $19/mo or $15/mo billed yearly. 2-month free trial, no card. Profit, PPC, stock and refund alerts in the app.
  • Ask questions about your profit: Sellerview Complete, $79/mo yearly ($99 monthly), with an AI Copilot. Basic is $15/mo yearly.
  • Profit plus PPC and alerts: tool4seller from $199/yr (1,000 orders/mo), with hijacking and Buy Box alerts on your phone.

FAQ

Can I see my Amazon profit in the Amazon Seller app?

The Amazon Seller app shows sales and orders. It does not take out your product cost, so it cannot show true net profit. A profit tool where you enter COGS can.

Are these phone apps free?

The apps are free to download. You sign in with your account on the web. QMT is free up to 1,000 orders a month; the others need a paid plan after the trial.

Which app has the longest free trial?

sellerboard has a 2-month free trial with no card. QMT gives a 30-day Pro trial, tool4seller 14 days on Standard, and Sellerview 3 days.

Can I do setup in the app?

Mostly no. Add product costs and settings on the web; the app is for checking numbers and alerts on the go.
