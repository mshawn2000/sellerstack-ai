sellerboard vs Helium 10 Profits

Short answer
If profit tracking is the only job, sellerboard is the cheaper tool: from $19/mo ($15/mo yearly) for up to 3,000 orders, versus Helium 10 Diamond at $359/mo ($279/mo yearly), the plan where Helium 10 lists full profit and loss reporting. If you already pay for Diamond for keywords, listings and ads, its P&L is included, and a second subscription may be redundant.
Last checked
2026-10-14 · By Shawn Mai, AI Seller Index

We have not tested these tools hands-on; this page is built from each vendor's own pricing and feature pages plus our tool listings. Links on this page are plain (non-affiliate) links. Always confirm prices on the vendor site.

Which job are you paying for?

  • Profit only: a dedicated profit tool like sellerboard.
  • Profit + research: Helium 10 Platinum lists only "Limited profit reporting".
  • Whole suite (research, listings, ads, P&L): Helium 10 Diamond lists "Profit and loss reporting".

Who each fits

sellerboard — best for Amazon sellers who want a dedicated profit dashboard at a low entry price. Not for sellers who want keyword research or listing tools in the same subscription, or Shopify-first brands (our listed limitation: "Amazon-focused; UI basic").

Helium 10 Profits (Diamond) — best for sellers already on Diamond for research, listings and ads. Not for sellers who only need profit numbers (our listed limitation: "Broad but deep tiers get pricey; learning curve").

Keep both or drop one?

Running both makes sense only if you need Platinum-level research and want fuller profit reporting than Platinum lists, without paying for Diamond: sellerboard Standard + Helium 10 Platinum is $179 + $1,188 = $1,367/yr on yearly billing. If you are already on Diamond, its P&L may make sellerboard redundant.

FAQ

Does Helium 10 Platinum include Profits?

Helium 10's pricing page lists Platinum with "Limited profit reporting"; full "Profit and loss reporting" is listed on Diamond. Check the vendor page for details.

Is there a free trial?

sellerboard: "1 month free trial. No credit card required." Helium 10: a limited Free plan is mentioned in its pricing FAQ.

Which is cheaper per year?

On yearly billing, sellerboard Standard is $179/yr; Helium 10 Diamond is $3,348/yr.
